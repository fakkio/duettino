# Italian search phrases for the landing page

What Italian speakers actually type when they need what Duettino does, for the Italian landing page (`/it/`). Gathered on 2026-10-05 from Google autocomplete (`hl=it`, `gl=it`, ~90 seed queries) and Italian forum thread titles. Reddit was not reachable. Autocomplete proves a phrase is typed, not how often: the ranking is a judgement from how many suggestions each family produced and how early.

## Vocabulary

- **call / chiamata / riunione**: "call" is fully Italian at work; "chiamata" about as strong; "riunione" is Teams' own word. "videochiamata" leans to WhatsApp and phones: secondary only.
- **The PC's sound**: "audio del PC" in headlines; "audio interno" and "audio di sistema" (Windows' own Snipping Tool wording) as synonyms in the body.
- **Headphones**: "cuffie" (also "cuffie bluetooth"); "auricolari" barely registers; "casse" collides with "cassette".
- **Patterns**: "come …", "programma per …", "app per …", plus "gratis" and "windows 11/10"; "contemporaneamente" for "at the same time".
- **Names typed as-is**: Audacity (the main competitor in Italian searches), OBS, Voicemeeter, VB-Cable.

## Worth knowing

- **Stereo Mix is "Missaggio stereo"** on an Italian Windows, with many "non funziona / mancante / non appare" threads: the Italian "Why not Stereo Mix?" FAQ should use that name.
- **"registrare audio pc senza microfono"** is frequent: people think the microphone is the only way to capture sound.
- **Recording without consent is searched a lot** ("come registrare riunione teams senza autorizzazione", "registrare zoom senza consenso"). Don't target it; the legal FAQ answers it (Teams' own recording notifies everyone, Duettino doesn't).
- Windows' built-in recorder ("registratore vocale / di suoni windows 11", often "non funziona") records the microphone only: a possible comparison point.
